# Final release scope

The authoritative figure source is `Figures 07 30 2026.pdf`, included in this deposit as `figures/manuscript_full_figure_set_2026-07-30.pdf`.

Only computational panels present in that PDF are mapped in the reproducibility index. Standalone reviewer-response figures, alternative renderings of Fig. 1G or Fig. 2B, broad cell-cycle QC figures, five-group non-CXCR6 marker grids, double-positive frequency summaries, and HUMESS overview panels were excluded because they do not appear in the authoritative figure set.

Necessary lightweight intermediate metrics and statistical tables were retained even when the final assembled panel exists only in the full figure PDF.

Supplementary Fig. 13C is mapped as a gene-level paired pseudobulk edgeR panel. Supplementary Fig. 13D-E are laboratory experiments and are outside this computational release.
